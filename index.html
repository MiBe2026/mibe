About the brand.

MiBe's standard is to deliver the best achievable outcome on every commitment. Where perfection isn't practical, the focus shifts to what is genuinely achievable and delivering that well.

Good enough is good enough, but only if it's actually good enough.

The offering is straightforward: websites, documents, and applications, built properly and handed over finished. IPOS is the flagship project, but the same standard applies to anything MiBe takes on — from a one-page site to a full piece of software.

Built with Claude.ai and Claude Code doing the typing. The bad decisions are still all MiBe's own.

Early career

Graduate scheme. Then contracting.

Started on a graduate scheme doing software testing for a company that makes headlines for all the wrong reasons. Did that for two years, finished it, then went contracting. Financial institutions, mostly. Success came at a price though...

🔥 everything wasn't ok

2020

Mental Health.

Something broke during lockdown. Not the kind of thing you walk off, the kind where you have to make a choice about what comes next, and "more of the same" wasn't one of the options.

If you're going through something similar: reach out. Seriously. Mind and Samaritans exist for exactly this.

🧠 it gets better

Post-lockdown

CSCS card. Boots. A shovel.

Instead of going back to a desk, got a CSCS card and went to work on construction sites as a labourer. Started from the bottom — general labourer, then power tools, then skilled labourer. Turns out physical work and a change of scene does something good for the brain.

🏗️ actual fresh air and silica

Now

Material controller.

Moved into materials control, where the IT background started pulling its weight again. Looked at how sites manage materials. Decided it was a mess. Started building IPOS — initially as a hobby, currently as something that might actually matter.

🟠 this is the one

It's Probably On Site.

Materials management on construction sites is a mess.
Spreadsheets. WhatsApp. Guesswork.
IPOS is what happens when a software engineer gets a CSCS card
and decides to actually fix the problem.
This is MiBe's first long term project.

Know where it is

Track materials on site in real time. Not "probably by the hoarding." Actually on site, on a timber rack, in a compound.
