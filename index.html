About the brand.

MiBe's standard is to deliver the best achievable outcome on every commitment. Where perfection isn't practical, the focus shifts to what is genuinely achievable and delivering that well.

Good enough is good enough, but only if it's actually good enough.

The offering is straightforward — four things, all handled properly, nothing half-finished:

🌐

Websites

Small sites, built fast and built to load fast. No bloat, no fifteen tracking scripts.

📄

Documents

Generated paperwork, reports, exportable output — the boring stuff, done properly.

💻

Applications

Real tools, from a one-off script to a full system. Built to be used, not just demoed.

🛠️

IT support

Domain registration, email set up, general technical support — the stuff that eats an afternoon if you don't already know it.

University.

🎓 2:2 and proud

Early career

Graduate scheme. Then contracting.

Started on a graduate scheme doing software testing for a company that makes headlines for all the wrong reasons. Did that for two years, finished it, then went contracting. Financial institutions, mostly.

🔥 everything wasn't ok

2020

The reset.

2020 had other plans. Turned out "more of the same" wasn't actually on the table, so a few things got rebuilt from the ground up — priorities included.

If any of that sounds familiar, Mind and Samaritans are worth a look.

🔄 rebuilt, not broken

Post-lockdown

CSCS card. Boots. A shovel.

Instead of going back to a desk, got a CSCS card and went to work on construction sites as a labourer. Started from the bottom — general labourer, then power tools, then skilled labourer. Turns out physical work and a change of scene does something good for the brain.

🏗️ actual fresh air and silica

Now

Material controller.

Moved into materials control, where the IT background started pulling its weight again. Looked at how sites manage materials. Decided it was a mess. Started building something to fix it — initially as a hobby, currently as something that might actually matter.

🟠 this is the one

The flagship

IPOS — It's Probably On Site. Materials management on construction sites is a mess: spreadsheets, WhatsApp, guesswork. This is what happens when a software engineer gets a CSCS card and decides to actually fix the problem.

📍

Know where it is

Track materials on site in real time. Not "probably by the hoarding." Actually on site, on a timber rack, in a compound.

📦

Know what you have

Deliveries logged. Stock tracked. No more walking the site to find those 30 lengths of CLS.

🔧

Built by someone on site

Not an office lackey's idea of what site workers need.
Built by a site worker, for site workers.

In development, not for sale.

IPOS is a long term project designed for single operator use as an iPhone & iPad app, though if there is interest it could be expanded. Want to see it working? Get in touch.
